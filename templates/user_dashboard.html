Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to commonly asked questions about the CIVITRACK+ portal and how to manage your complaints effectively.

Common Queries

How to raise?

Go to the "Raise Complaint" section, select a department, and fill out the details including title and description.

Track Status?

Use the "Track Complaint" section. Enter your Tracking ID and the captcha to see the current status of your issue.

Can I edit?

Yes, you can edit complaints that are still 'Pending'. Go to "Edit Complaint" to make changes to your submission.

Deletion?

Deleting a complaint removes it permanently from the system. Use "Delete Complaint" if the issue is no longer relevant.

Time frame?

Resolution time varies by department. You will receive updates as the status changes from Pending to Resolved.

Departments?

We support various departments including PWD, Health, Electricity, and more for comprehensive civic tracking.
