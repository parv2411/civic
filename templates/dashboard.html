About CIVITRACK+

Our Mission

CIVITRACK+ is a unified civic management system designed to bridge the gap between citizens and local authorities. Our platform empowers communities to report civic issues like road damage, water leaks, garbage collection, and power outages efficiently. Track your complaint status in real-time and help improve your community.

Key Features

  • Easy complaint registration with photo evidence
  • Real-time complaint tracking system
  • Multi-department support (PWD, Water, Municipality, Green Services)
  • Multilingual interface (English & Malayalam)
  • Transparent citizen-government engagement
